Meet Paddo: The Cute Alien Who’s Crash-Landing into Better Atoms

Hold onto your helmets, Earthlings!

At Better Atoms Technologies LLP, we believe that science shouldn't just be memorized—it should be played with. We believe that the best way to learn isn’t by getting the right answer instantly, but by making a glorious, spectacular mess along the way.

But we couldn't explore the universe alone. We needed a Captain.

We looked for someone brave. We looked for someone smart. Instead... we found Paddo.

👽 Who is Paddo?

Paddo is a space explorer from the Andromeda Galaxy. He is small. He is green. And he has a very bad habit of thinking he knows everything (spoiler alert: he doesn’t).

Along with his co-pilot Spark—a holographic AI who is obsessed with rules and silence—Paddo travels the universe in his ship, the Starhopper. He came to Earth for a quiet vacation, but thanks to some "unexpected data" (Spark calls them crashes), he’s here to stay.

Paddo is the official mascot and face of Better Atoms Technologies. He represents the curious kid in all of us: the one who pushes the button just to see what happens, and the one who learns that "failure" is just another word for "gathering data."

📚 The Adventure Begins: Paddo and the Trial of Curiosity

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our very first Read-Aloud STEM Adventure: "Better Atoms: Paddo and the Trial of Curiosity."

In this debut book, Paddo and Spark team up with two Earth kids, Leo and Maya, to save the planet from the Universal Accountants—a group of cosmic bureaucrats who want to delete Earth because it’s too "messy and inefficient."

Inside the book, your kids will:

🚀 Launch rockets using Newton’s Laws (and balloons).

🥪 Power a spaceship using the chemical energy of a ham sandwich.

💎 Escape a Diamond Planet where gravity is too strong.

🔊 Open doors by singing (Resonance).

Why Paddo? Why Now?

We created Paddo to solve a problem. Too many kids think science is just for "smart people" in white coats. They think if they get an answer wrong, they aren't good at it.

Paddo changes that. Paddo messes up. He mistakes dangerous chemicals for "pretty colors." He forgets to calculate fuel. But with the help of science (and his human friends), he fixes it.

He teaches children that:

Curiosity is stronger than gravity.

Mistakes are the recipe for discovery.

The universe is full of "unexpected data," and that's what makes it fun.

📝 Captain’s Profile

Name: Paddo

Rank: Captain of the Starhopper

Home: Andromeda Galaxy

Favorite Food: Anything containing Carbon.

Weakness: Overconfidence; fancy gravity boots.

Best Friend: Spark (though Spark denies this).

Mission: To make the invisible universe visible for kids everywhere.

Ready to Launch?

Paddo is ready to take your child on a journey from the breakfast table to the Red Giants of deep space.

"Better Atoms: Paddo and the Trial of Curiosity" is more than a book—it’s a training manual for the future captains of the universe. Packed with hands-on experiments (using safe kitchen items!), it turns storytime into science time.

Welcome to the crew, Paddo. Let’s make some waves.
