Paddo and the Trial of Curiosity

The Space Explorer's Handbook

A read-aloud STEM adventure for ages 6 to 12. Book 1 of The Better Atoms Adventures. By Aman Singh.

Get it on Amazon

To the stargazers, the question-askers, and the future captains of the universe. May your curiosity always be stronger than gravity.

16 chapters, 16 science ideas

  1. 1. The Day the Air Stopped Moving

    Air pressure: bouncing atoms push

  2. 2. The Library of Lights

    Protons decide what an atom is; atoms bond

  3. 3. The Sandwich That Saved Us

    Food is stored chemical energy

  4. 4. The Planet That Pulled Too Hard

    Mass vs weight; denser planets pull harder

  5. 5. The Meltdown at the Red Giant

    Heat: conductors vs insulators

  6. 6. The Launch Without Fuel

    Every action has an equal and opposite reaction

  7. 7. The Maze of Mirrors

    Light reflects and bends

  8. 8. The Singing Crystals

    Sound is vibration; resonance

  9. 9. The Magnetic Mountain

    Magnets have poles; Earth is a magnet

  10. 10. The Glass World

    Closed ecosystems and the water cycle

  11. 11. The Wire That Woke Up

    Circuits and static electricity

  12. 12. The Slide to Nowhere

    Friction

  13. 13. The Door That Wouldn't Budge

    Levers trade distance for force

  14. 14. The Secret Whisper

    Sound waves travel through solids

  15. 15. The Pattern in the Stars

    Heavy objects bend space (black holes)

  16. 16. The Final Exam

    Energy from waste; creativity counts

Every activity in the book needs active adult supervision.
